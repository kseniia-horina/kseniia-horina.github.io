How I work

Quality before the first line of code, not after the last

Ahead of development

I review user stories with product owners before a sprint starts — checking that requirements are complete, testable and actually match what the client asked for. Most of the defects I prevent never get written down, because they never get built.

During development

Test strategy, planning and hundreds of test plans and test cases. I sit inside the code-review and PR-approval flow rather than waiting at the end of it, and I take critical findings back to root cause instead of filing the symptom.

At release

Release validation and sign-off, running up to five products at once by staggering cycles and prioritising by risk. Routine regression and smoke go to Claude, ChatGPT and mabl; the time that frees up goes to edge cases and new features.

Across the team

I onboarded and mentored two QA engineers (web and mobile), ran product demos for stakeholders, wrote documentation standards later projects reused, and handed TelCloud over to a new tester when I moved on.
