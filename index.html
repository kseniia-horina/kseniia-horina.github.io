How I work

Quality before the first line of code, not after the last

01

Ahead of development

I review user stories with product owners before a sprint starts — checking that requirements are complete, testable and actually match what the client asked for. Most of the defects I prevent never get written down, because they never get built. Lately that includes business-analysis work: validating Mixpanel tracking and whether the data collected supports the analysis it's meant for.

02

During development

Test strategy, risk-based planning and test-case libraries with traceability. REST APIs get tested in Postman and Swagger, with regression checks automated as scripted Postman collections. I sit inside the code-review and PR-approval flow rather than waiting at the end of it, and take critical findings back to root cause instead of filing the symptom.

03

At release

Release validation and sign-off across up to five parallel release streams, closing up to 30 tickets a day at peak. Routine regression and smoke go to Claude, ChatGPT and mabl, and I'm automating recurring checks such as price-display validation; the time that frees up goes to edge cases.

04

Across the team

QA Lead for the Sensei Cloud team: I set testing priorities, run cross-team QA syncs on best practices and AI tools, and give sprint updates to leadership. I've onboarded and mentored 10 QA engineers, taught developers Jenkins, and I'm building a tool that automates Jenkins builds and promotions.
